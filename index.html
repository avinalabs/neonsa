THE TOWER

Five decks stacked 2,900px high. Every deck has its own flippers, its own toys and its own danger. Fall a floor and you get another shot — fall out the bottom and the ball is gone.
ALTITUDE = MULTIPLIER. Gutter x1 → Crown x8.

CLIMB

RAMPS fling you up a floor — shoot up the chevrons, they are not holes, and a slow or side-on shot rattles back at you · PORTALS jump you across · MAGNETS grab and launch · CANNON aims itself at the Crown · MYSTERY pays a random award — points, multiball, an extra ball, a power orb, a multiplier.
MISSIONS at the Spine scoop. Four of them lights THE STORM KING. Beat him for ASCENSION.

THE THREE GATES

Three holes in the tower are not holes. Fall down one and you are somewhere else entirely — a different machine, different gravity, different everything.
SPIRAL a turning room that will not sit still
FORGE climb, the floor is molten and rising
ABYSS a third of gravity, and currents
You cannot lose a ball down there. Clear the room for the jackpot, then the gate goes cold for a while.
